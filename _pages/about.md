# About

I am an undergraduate student in [Computer Science and Engineering](https://cs.korea.ac.kr/cs/index.do) at [Korea University](https://www.korea.edu/). I am currently a research intern at the [MLAI Lab](https://www.mlai-kaist.com/) at [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/), where I am advised by Prof. [Sung Ju Hwang](http://www.sungjuhwang.com/).

**My research focuses on developing more capable, efficient, and adaptive LLM-based systems.** I am particularly interested in how language models and agents can reason, act, and accumulate knowledge efficiently over long horizons, while minimizing unnecessary computation, token usage, and external retrieval. My recent interests include token-efficient LLM agents, memory and knowledge integration, and parametric mechanisms for efficient adaptation.
